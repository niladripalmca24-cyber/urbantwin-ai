End-to-End Pipeline Architecture

From edge video ingest to holographic 3D digital twin synchronization and predictive control.

STEP 01

Edge Vision Ingest

Multi-camera RTSP video feeds processed with YOLOv8 at 30+ FPS for vehicle bounding boxes & directional lane flux.

STEP 02

Cryptographic Hashing

Edge ANPR extracts license plate strings and securely salts them into irreversible SHA-256 tokens before network transmission.

STEP 03

Graph Spatial Fusion

Cross-camera tracking matches vehicle re-identification and feeds topological road graph with real-time lane densities.

STEP 04

3D Twin & Simulation

Live WebGL 3D city syncs with GCN forecasts and empowers traffic controllers to simulate signal timing & road rerouting.
